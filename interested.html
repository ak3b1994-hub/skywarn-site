Get Involved / Become a Spotter

Baltimore City · Baltimore County · Carroll County · Howard County

Skywarn is the National Weather Service's volunteer storm spotter program. Trained spotters report what they actually see on the ground — hail size, wind damage, flooding, rotation — so forecasters at NWS Baltimore/Washington can issue better, faster warnings. Radar can't see what you can.

What spotters do

  • Watch the sky during severe weather and report conditions that meet NWS criteria
  • Relay reports by amateur radio during a Skywarn net, or directly to NWS online or by phone
  • Help confirm or correct what radar is showing — your report can change a warning

You do not need a ham radio license to be a spotter. A license lets you check into our nets and report in real time, but anyone who completes the training can report.

How to get started

  1. Take the free NWS trainingAttend a Skywarn Basics class (in person or virtual) through NWS Baltimore/Washington, or complete the online course on COMET MetEd.
  2. Join our DiscordThat's where activations are announced, nets are coordinated, and new spotters get connected with their county's net control operators.
  3. Learn the netsCheck the repeater list and net scripts on the homepage. Listen in during an activation before you check in.
